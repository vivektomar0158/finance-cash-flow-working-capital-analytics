# Assumptions

- The forecast starts on the configured Monday as-of date and covers 13 seven-day weeks.
- Paid invoices use actual payment dates; open invoices use expected payment dates.
- Overdue open invoices are collected in the first base-case week.
- The stress case delays only unpaid customer receipts by 20 calendar days.
- Supplier and other payments remain unchanged under stress.
- Minimum cash is a policy threshold, not a bank overdraft limit.
- All values are illustrative and denominated in INR.
