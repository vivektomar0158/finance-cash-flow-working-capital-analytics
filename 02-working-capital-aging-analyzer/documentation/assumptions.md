# Assumptions

- Reporting date is 2026-01-05 and the standard period is 30 days.
- Partially paid invoices and bills remain open for their unpaid balance.
- DSO, DPO, and inventory days use ending balances because the sample contains one reporting snapshot.
- The five-day DSO improvement is an estimate of cash released, not a guaranteed collection result.
- Risk scores are transparent and combine overdue percentage, aging, credit utilization, and concentration.
- All values are illustrative and denominated in INR.
